# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2025**

## Summary

AI Code App is a **local-first** desktop application. Your code, conversations, and API keys never leave your device except when sent to the AI provider you choose.

## What we collect

**Nothing is sent to us.** All data stays on your machine:

- **API keys** — Stored locally in encrypted storage on your device. Sent only to the AI provider you configure (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or your custom endpoint).
- **Conversations** — Stored locally on your device. Never uploaded anywhere.
- **Code & files** — Read from your workspace folder. Sent to your AI provider as part of tool calls you initiate. Never sent anywhere else.
- **Usage telemetry** — Optional, local-only analytics stored in a JSON file on your device. Tracks feature usage counts (no PII, no code, no conversations). Never transmitted externally.

## Third-party services

When you use the app, your messages are sent to the AI provider you configure:

| Provider | Privacy policy |
|----------|---------------|
| Anthropic (Claude) | https://www.anthropic.com/privacy |
| OpenAI (GPT) | https://openai.com/privacy |
| Google (Gemini) | https://ai.google.dev/terms |
| Custom/Ollama | Depends on your endpoint — local endpoints never leave your machine |

## Data you can delete

All app data is stored in your operating system's user data directory:

- **Windows:** `%APPDATA%/ai-code-app/`
- **macOS:** `~/Library/Application Support/ai-code-app/`
- **Linux:** `~/.config/ai-code-app/`

Delete this folder to remove all app data, settings, and conversation history.

## Auto-updates

The app checks for updates via GitHub Releases. No personal data is transmitted during update checks.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
